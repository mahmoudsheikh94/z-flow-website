## The Problem: Your Company's Knowledge is Scattered

Every company accumulates years of knowledge — in documents, emails, Slack messages, meeting notes, and most critically, in people's heads. When someone leaves, that knowledge walks out the door. When a new employee joins, they spend weeks asking the same questions.

This is the problem Company Brain solves.

## What is a Company Brain?

A Company Brain is an AI system trained on your company's own data. Think of it as a knowledgeable colleague who has read every document, every SOP, every customer email — and can answer questions about any of it instantly.

Unlike generic AI tools like ChatGPT, a Company Brain:

- **Knows your business** — trained on your actual documents, processes, and history
- **Speaks your language** — uses your company's terminology and context
- **Stays current** — automatically updates as new documents and data come in
- **Respects access controls** — only shows information users are authorized to see

## How It Works (The Technical Side)

Under the hood, a Company Brain uses a technology called **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**. Here's the simplified version:

1. **Ingestion** — Your documents (PDFs, Word files, SOPs, wiki pages) are processed and converted into searchable chunks
2. **Embedding** — Each chunk is converted into a mathematical representation (a vector) that captures its meaning
3. **Storage** — These vectors are stored in a specialized vector database for fast retrieval
4. **Query** — When someone asks a question, the system finds the most relevant chunks and feeds them to an AI model
5. **Response** — The AI generates an answer based specifically on your company's data, with source references

The key difference from simply throwing documents into ChatGPT: RAG systems can handle thousands of documents, stay up to date automatically, and cite their sources.
